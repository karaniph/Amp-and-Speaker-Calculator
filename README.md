## ✨ Overview

Choosing the right power amplifier for a speaker system can be confusing. Wattage, impedance, SPL, listening distance, speaker configuration, and headroom all affect how an amplifier should be matched to a system.

The **Power Amplifier Calculator (Car & PA)** helps simplify amplifier sizing by calculating the power requirements for different speaker setups.

Whether you're planning a car audio system, sizing a PA amplifier, matching a home hi-fi system, or working with guitar and acoustic setups, the calculator helps you work through the numbers before buying or installing an amplifier.

Instead of relying only on amplifier wattage ratings or guesswork, the tool helps you understand how the amplifier and speaker system should work together.

## 📖 How to Use

1. **Install the Extension:** Download directly from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/power-amplifier-calculato/dcckpfcpjjpmcoojclkmhckjifgpllfo).

2. **Launch the Extension:** Click the **Power Amplifier Calculator** icon in your browser toolbar.

3. **Select Your Application:** Choose the type of system you're working with, such as Car Audio, Pro Audio / PA, Home Audio, Guitar, or Acoustic.

4. **Enter Your System Requirements:** Enter the relevant speaker, impedance, SPL, listening-distance, and other available system information.

5. **Calculate Amplifier Requirements:** Run the calculation to estimate the amplifier power required for your setup.

6. **Review Headroom & Impedance:** Check the recommended power and impedance information before selecting an amplifier.

7. **Compare Amplifier Options:** Use the calculated requirements as a reference when comparing available amplifiers.

## 💡 Why Use a Power Amplifier Calculator?

Choosing an amplifier isn't simply about finding the amplifier with the highest wattage rating.

The appropriate amplifier depends on factors such as:

* Speaker power handling
* Speaker impedance
* Number of speakers
* Target SPL
* Listening distance
* System type
* Required headroom

A very powerful amplifier isn't automatically the right amplifier, while an amplifier with too little available power may not provide the performance or headroom required by the system.

The calculator brings these considerations together so users can make a more informed decision before purchasing or installing an amplifier.

## 🔊 Understanding Amplifier Power

Amplifier specifications can be confusing because manufacturers may advertise different power ratings under different test conditions.

When comparing amplifiers, it is important to look beyond the headline wattage and consider:

* RMS / continuous power
* Speaker impedance
* Number of channels
* Speaker configuration
* Target SPL
* Listening distance
* Available headroom

The Power Amplifier Calculator is designed to help users work through these considerations and establish a sensible starting point for amplifier selection.

## ⚠️ Important Note

The calculator provides estimates and planning guidance based on the information entered by the user.

Actual system performance can vary depending on speaker sensitivity, enclosure design, room or vehicle acoustics, amplifier characteristics, installation conditions, gain structure, and other real-world factors.

Always verify amplifier and speaker specifications against the manufacturer's documentation before installation or operation.
